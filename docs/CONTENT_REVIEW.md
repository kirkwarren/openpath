# Curriculum review rubric

Current lessons are original samples awaiting educator review. They are not a complete curriculum or a validated mastery assessment. The provenance field must remain `needs-educator-review`; approval is recorded through an attributable human PR review.

For each content PR, a reviewer should record:

1. Intended age/grade, skill, prerequisites, learning objective, and relevant curriculum context. Avoid implying all countries follow one standard.
2. Independent verification of every factual claim, calculation, answer key, and explanation. Verify that exactly one option is defensibly correct.
3. Developmental suitability, reading load, accessibility, inclusivity, and cultural assumptions. No manipulative messaging, unrelated persuasion, product promotion, or discriminatory framing.
4. Safe practical activities with appropriate adult involvement; no requests for personal information, private contact, purchases, downloads, or off-platform interaction.
5. Originality, attribution, permissions, and MIT compatibility. Sources belong in the review record; contributed text must not smuggle links or executable instructions into lessons.
6. Hints that support reasoning, plausible distractors, and honest limits on what a correct response demonstrates. More than one question and delayed review are needed for meaningful mastery claims.
7. For translations, a fluent human review of meaning, answer equivalence, local context, and reading level. Machine translation alone is insufficient.

Record reviewer identity, relevant experience, scope reviewed, findings, and remaining uncertainties. A checkbox, bot comment, or generated “approved” field is not sufficient evidence. Maintainers should defer release of material they cannot competently assess.
