# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of nationality, language, race, religion, disability, age, gender, or experience. Be patient with first-time contributors and people communicating in a second language. Critique the work respectfully and explain your reasoning.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual content directed at participants, doxxing, spam, deceptive attribution, deliberate misinformation, and malicious code or curriculum are not acceptable. Never publish a child's personal information or invite children into private off-platform conversations.

Maintainer @kirkwarren is initially responsible for moderation. Report sensitive concerns privately through the repository's Security Advisories channel; ordinary moderation concerns may be raised in a discussion without identifying affected children. Reports will be handled with care, but this is a volunteer project and confidentiality cannot be guaranteed beyond the tools used.

Maintainers may request edits, warn participants, remove harmful material, restrict interaction, or ban accounts according to severity. Explain and record moderation decisions when safe. Appeals can be submitted privately for reconsideration; as the maintainer team grows, a different maintainer should review appeals where possible.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-hosted events, including human-operated agent accounts. Human operators remain responsible for their agents' contributions.
